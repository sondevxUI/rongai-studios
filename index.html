From request to gallery

How it works

  1. Send a request

    Pick a package, a date and a time of day. A request does not charge you anything.

  2. We confirm

    We contact you by phone or WhatsApp to agree the date, the final price and any other details.

  3. Come to the studio

    Your session runs for the time in your package, at Mikao House in Rongai Tumaini.

  4. Get your gallery

    Your edited photos are shared with you in an online gallery.
